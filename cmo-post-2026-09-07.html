I think most UAE SME owners don't know their Corporate Tax filing date until someone else brings it up first. A client called us three weeks ago asking if she still had time before her CT return was due. She didn't. She had 23 days. Her bookkeeper had the numbers sitting in a spreadsheet, not built into an actual return. Nobody had prepared the schedules the FTA needs to see. We rebuilt it and filed with time to check it twice, not once. That is the real difference. Having numbers is not the same as having a filing ready to submit. The Corporate Tax return deadline is 30 September 2026. Miss it and the penalty is AED 500 a month, every month, until it's filed. That is not a warning. That is what happens. Most owners think Corporate Tax is something their accountant "handles." I think that assumption is exactly how a business ends up 23 days out with a spreadsheet instead of a return. This is why we think like owners here. We run a second set of eyes over every filing before it's submitted, because a mistake in a CT return costs more to fix later than it does to catch now. If a client loses a dirham to a penalty we could have caught, that comes from our side too. If you're not certain your CT return is ready today, that's worth finding out this week, not the week it's due. Book a 30-minute compliance review. P.S. The FTA doesn't care that your accountant meant to get to it.
