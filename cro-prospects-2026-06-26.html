QFZP All-or-Nothing Risk (MD 229/2025, retroactive 1 Jun 2023): A free zone company loses its 0% Corporate Tax status entirely if it fails any single qualifying condition. The 9% CT applies to all income for that year plus four subsequent years. MD 229 of 2025 expanded qualifying commodity categories but the all-or-nothing structure is unchanged.

E-Invoicing Deadline (31 July 2026): Businesses with AED 50M+ revenue must appoint an FTA Accredited Service Provider by 31 July 2026. Mandatory e-invoicing begins 1 January 2027. Smaller businesses should begin system preparation now.

AML / goAML (Ministry of Economy, 2026): Real estate brokers, accountants, and corporate service providers classified as DNFBPs must register on the goAML portal and renew annually. Non-registration is a compliance breach under UAE AML law.

Free Zone Audit Requirement (All major zones, 2026): 98% of UAE free zones require audited financial statements annually regardless of size or revenue. DMCC, JAFZA, DIFC, and DAFZA maintain closed approved-auditor lists. Submissions from unlisted firms are rejected at licence renewal.
