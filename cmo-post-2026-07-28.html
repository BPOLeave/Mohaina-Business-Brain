31 July. That's it.
If your business missed the CIT registration deadline, the FTA opened a penalty waiver window. It closes Thursday. Here's what most business owners don't know: The standard late-registration penalty is AED 10,000. It applies the moment the window closes. But inside this window — file now, and that penalty gets automatically waived. You don't appeal it. You don't negotiate it. You just have to move before Thursday. I've seen this play out with clients already this month. One filed last week. No penalty. Done. The ones who wait until Thursday morning and find their supporting documents aren't ready — that's a different conversation. What to check before 31 July: → Has your entity filed its first CIT return, or just registered? → Are your books in order enough to support the filing? → Do you have your Trade Licence, MoA, and financial statements ready? Registration is not the same as filing. Both have deadlines. Both have consequences. If you're not sure where you stand — this week. Not next week.
DM me "CIT" before Thursday and I'll tell you exactly where your exposure is.
