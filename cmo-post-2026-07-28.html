Full Post · Copy-Ready

A payments startup owner thought he knew his revenue. The real number was hiding inside a different account entirely.

His Zoho Books looked clean. Monthly reconciliations done, nothing obviously wrong on the surface.

But when we sat down with him, the number he thought was his revenue was not his revenue. It was merchant settlement float, money moving through the account before it was actually his to count.

I think this is the trap growing FinTech businesses fall into more than most. Payments hit the bank, the owner sees the balance go up, and it feels like income. It is not. It is client money sitting there until it clears and settles.

Because nobody had separated the two, his CIT registration timing got missed. Not because he was careless. Because he could not see what his own numbers were actually telling him until we traced every transaction back to its source.

We went in, sat with his team, and rebuilt the chart of accounts so settlement float and real revenue live in separate places, properly tagged, properly reported. Now when he looks at the dashboard, he sees his business, not someone else's money passing through it.

At the end of the day, the FTA does not care that the confusion was innocent. They care what the books actually say. We would rather catch this in a working session at his office than have him find out from a penalty notice.

Tag a founder who needs to see this.
