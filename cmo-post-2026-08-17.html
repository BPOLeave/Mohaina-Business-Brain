On time and correct are not the same thing. 11 days until your VAT return is due. Here's the checklist we run before any client's return goes to the FTA portal. 1. Reconcile the VAT account to the general ledger. If the two don't match, don't file yet. 2. Check every input tax claim against the actual invoice, not a summary sheet. 3. Confirm zero-rated and exempt supplies are coded correctly. Mixing these up is the error we catch most. 4. Match the sales register to actual bank credits for the period. 5. Review credit notes issued this quarter. They belong in the same period as the original invoice. 6. Run the reverse charge entries separately. The FTA reads these as two transactions, not one. 7. Second eyes on the return before it's submitted. Whoever prepared it should not be the one who signs off. 8. Screenshot the FTA portal confirmation. Keep it in your working file, not just your inbox. Once it's on the FTA portal, you cannot change it again. Miss one of these and the FTA doesn't read it as a correction. It reads as a penalty, then it follows you into next year's CIT filing. Late filing is AED 1,000 the first time, AED 2,000 if it happens again. Late payment adds 2% immediately, then 4% a month after that. We think like owners. If a client loses a dirham, it's something that also comes from our pocket too. P.S. Book a 30-minute compliance review before the 28th. We'll catch what the checklist won't.
