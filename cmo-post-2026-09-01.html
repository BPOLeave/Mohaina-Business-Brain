I think the scariest gaps are the ones that don't show up anywhere. We onboarded a professional services firm a few months ago. Solid team, steady clients, five years in business. Their books looked clean. Monthly reports filed, VAT returns on time, everything appeared in order. Then we went through their file document by document, the way we do with every new client before we touch a single number. Their Corporate Tax registration was never filed. Not late. Not incomplete. Missing entirely. Their previous accountant had assumed someone else handled it. The owner assumed it was done because nobody had told him otherwise. We caught it during onboarding, not during an FTA review. We registered them that week. Correct filings, a clear paper trail, documentation showing exactly when and how it was resolved. The owner didn't get angry. He went quiet, then said something I still think about. "I would never have known to ask." Compliance gaps rarely announce themselves. They sit quietly in a file until someone goes looking, or until the FTA does it for you. We go through every new client's documents before we touch a single report. Not because we assume something is wrong. Because sometimes it is, and nobody told them. Save this for the situation where you find out your CT status was never confirmed, not from a warning letter, but from someone who actually checked. P.S. He didn't need a warning letter. He needed someone to actually look.
