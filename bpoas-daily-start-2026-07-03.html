Compliance Without the Panic Friday · 3 July 2026 4/4 Invisibility Diagnostic Pending Approval
If eInvoicing went live tomorrow, here are the 5 gaps I would close first:

1. No compliant accounting system.
The FTA mandate requires invoices to be generated and transmitted through an FTA-approved, integrated system. WhatsApp PDFs and Excel sheets will not be accepted. If you are still on manual invoicing, Zoho Books and Odoo are both FTA-integration-ready. This is the foundational fix.

2. Master data that does not match your trade licence.
Your TRN, legal entity name, and registered address must match exactly across every invoice. Any mismatch — even a name abbreviation — risks rejection. Clean this before the mandate.

3. Tax codes not mapped at the item level.
Zero-rated, exempt, out-of-scope, and standard-rated transactions must be mapped per item in your system. A catch-all rate corrected at the VAT return level will not hold when your system transmits structured data.

4. No FTA portal integration.
Your accounting software needs to connect directly to the FTA system for real-time invoice transmission. If your current software does not support this, switch now — not the week the mandate goes live.

5. No test invoicing cycle.
Most businesses that think they are ready find a gap on the first live test. Run one before the mandate. You want to find the problem, not be the problem.

The businesses that move before the mandate will spend less than the ones that wait.
