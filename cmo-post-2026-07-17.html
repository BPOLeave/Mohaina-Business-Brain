Full Post · Copy-Ready

Your bank balance is not a P&L. If that's the only number you check, you don't actually know how your business is doing.

I think most owners open their banking app before they open anything else. It feels like the fastest way to know if things are okay.

We had a client running a trading company that way for two years. Good balance most months, so he assumed the business was healthy. When we went through his books, he had close to AED 300,000 sitting in receivables he had never chased, and a VAT liability building up that his bank balance never showed him.

His bank balance wasn't wrong. It just wasn't telling him the whole story.

Here are the 3 numbers we tell every new client to check monthly, alongside the bank balance, not instead of it.

1. Accounts receivable aging. Not just what's owed to you, but how much of it is 30, 60, 90 days overdue. Money sitting unclaimed isn't cash, no matter what the bank shows.

2. Net profit, not revenue. A good sales month can still be a losing month once costs, salaries, and tax obligations are counted.

3. Upcoming liabilities. VAT, CIT, payroll, supplier payments due in the next 30 days. Today's balance says nothing about what's already spoken for.

At the end of the day, cash in the bank is one number. What the numbers are telling you, all of them, is what actually keeps a business safe.
