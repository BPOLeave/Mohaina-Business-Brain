The FTA's "awareness phase" for Corporate Tax is over. Nobody told most SME owners. I think most SME owners still believe the FTA is going easy on Corporate Tax mistakes. That was true for the first filing cycle. It is not true anymore. From January this year, the Tax Procedures Law changed. The FTA has wider powers now, tighter deadlines, and a digital system that flags non-compliance automatically. No inspector has to notice you. The system does that on its own. Here is what that means in practice. If your financial year runs on the calendar year, 30 September is your Corporate Tax filing and payment deadline. Miss it, and the penalty is AED 10,000, instantly. After that, the FTA charges 14% per annum on whatever you owe, with no ceiling. On AED 500,000 in tax, three months late already costs you around AED 17,500 in penalties alone, on top of the tax itself. We think like owners, so here is what we tell every client with a calendar year end. Do not wait until September to find out what you owe. Reconcile your books now, confirm your taxable profit, and file with room to spare. Second eyes on the numbers before the FTA's system sees them first. The FTA is not asking anymore. It is watching. Book a 30-minute compliance review. P.S. AED 10,000 is the starting number, not the ending one.
