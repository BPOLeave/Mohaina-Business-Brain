Three government emails in 48 hours. No client should be in that position. One from MOHRE. A worker's documents are missing at the Ministry of Higher Education — missing degree recognition. The email arrived Sunday. Then again Monday morning. One from the FTA. A formal reminder that a corporate tax return is still pending. The deadline is approaching. One from the UAE IEC. Two entries on the ISIL Daesh and AlQaida Sanctions Committee list were amended. Per Cabinet Decision 74 of 2020, every business in the UAE is required to implement this update. Each one alone — manageable. All three in the same week — that is a pattern. I think what most business owners do not realise is that compliance is not just VAT anymore. It is MOHRE. It is FTA. It is AML obligations. It is HR records. It is supporting documents. It is all running at the same time. When one thing slips, the others do not wait. The businesses that stay ahead of government notifications are the ones that have someone checking all of it — not just the books.
