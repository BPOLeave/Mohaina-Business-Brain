I almost let our own trade license renewal slip past me last week. The one deadline we tell every client never to lose track of, and we nearly lost track of it ourselves. Ours renews on 25 July. Five days out, someone on the team flagged it, not me. That's the part that stuck with me. It happens because a trade license doesn't show up on a VAT return or a CIT filing. It just sits quietly on a certificate until the date is almost on top of you. We caught ours in time. Now every client gets the same 4-date tracker we built for BPOAS after that morning: 1. Trade license expiry, the one nobody puts on a calendar because it only comes once a year 2. VAT return, due the same day every quarter, no exceptions, no grace period 3. CIT filing, once a year, but the penalty doesn't feel like once a year 4. AML or DNFBP filing, if your business activity is regulated A lapsed license doesn't just cost a fine. It can freeze your bank account and your team's visas along with it. If you're the one holding all four of these dates in your head instead of on a system, you're carrying a risk you don't need to carry. Second eyes catch what one person misses. That's true for your books. It's true for your renewals too. Book a 30-minute compliance review. P.S. If you're not sure when your own trade license renews, that's the first thing to check today. Not next week.
