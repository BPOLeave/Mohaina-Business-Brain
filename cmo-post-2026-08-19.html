The eInvoicing date everyone's rushing to beat isn't the one that applies to most UAE SMEs. We've had a few conversations this month with owners convinced they're already behind. The Ministry of Finance moved the eInvoicing service provider deadline to 30 October 2026, and everyone quoting that date is talking about businesses doing AED 50 million or more a year. If that's not your revenue, that date isn't yours yet. Here's what actually is yours. The mandate itself goes live 1 January 2027 for those larger businesses first. Smaller UAE SMEs are expected to follow around mid 2027. Most owners we talk to are already anxious about FTA penalties without knowing if they're even compliant, and confusing which date applies to them only adds to that. I think the real risk for most owners isn't missing an October date that was never theirs. It's waking up next year with a live mandate and a system that was never built to talk to the FTA portal. Zoho Books and Odoo both need proper setup time, a clean chart of accounts, correct invoice formatting, and integration testing. That's not something you do the week the mandate lands. We're already doing this groundwork with a few clients now, not because their deadline is close, but because being ready before it's urgent costs less than being ready after. For anyone who wants the exact mechanics: the ASP appointment deadline moved under an amendment to Ministerial Decision No. 244 of 2025, and it's specifically tied to the AED 50 million revenue threshold.
