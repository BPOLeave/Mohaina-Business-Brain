Full Post Text (1,382 characters)

The most expensive accountant I have ever seen a business hire was also the cheapest one. He was paying his secretary a few hundred dirhams extra a month to handle the books alongside her actual job. No qualifications, no real system, just someone willing to do it cheap. For two years the owner thought he was saving money. Then we came in to review his books before a VAT filing. His secretary had been closing periods wrong, missing input tax that was fully claimable, and marking invoices as paid when they were not. The reconstruction alone cost him more than three years of what he thought he was saving. I think most owners still see accounting as a line item to cut, not a decision that protects everything else they built. We are dedicated in a way that makes us feel that if a client loses a dirham, it is something that also comes from our pocket. A cheap accountant who does not think that way is not actually cheap. You just have not been billed for the mistake yet. We go to a client's office, check the supporting documents physically, and a second person reviews every submission before it goes to the FTA. Nobody in our process is quietly guessing on your behalf. At the end of the day, the real cost of an accountant was never the invoice you paid them. It is the one you did not see coming. Tag a founder who needs to see this before their next VAT filing.
