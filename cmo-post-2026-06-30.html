3 months sounds like a lot. It isn't. Your CIT return is due 30 September. In June, that date feels manageable. By August, when you are still chasing missing invoices, trying to reconcile two years of accounts, and working out what transfer pricing documentation actually requires, it won't. If you are claiming the 0% qualifying income rate under Article 18, you must pass the substance test. Not assume it. A tax group decision if you have multiple entities. Step 4 — Pinned Comments (post immediately after main post)
Comment 1 · Extend — UAE compliance stat / law reference
Under Article 53 of the UAE Corporate Tax Law, the FTA can request supporting records for up to 7 years after a return has been filed. That means your first CIT filing needs to be supported by documentation going back to the first day of your first tax period — not just the invoices you pull together in September. If your books were not properly maintained from day one of your tax year, that is a reconstruction project, not a filing project. The earlier you identify this, the cheaper it is to fix.
