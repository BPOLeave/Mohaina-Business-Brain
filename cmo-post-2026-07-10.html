Full Post · Copy-Ready

Before you open WhatsApp Monday morning, close it. Check these three numbers first. Every Monday, a message is waiting before you have even had coffee. A client asking about an invoice. A supplier asking about a payment. A team member asking about payroll. It is tempting to answer straight away and sort out the numbers later. We tell clients to flip that order. Three numbers, checked before anything else, tell you more about the week ahead than the bank balance ever will. 1. What you actually collected last week, not what you invoiced. Invoicing and collecting are two different events. An invoice sent is not cash in hand. If the gap between the two is growing, that is the first thing worth noticing. 2. What is due out in the next seven days. Payroll, supplier payments, VAT if it is filing month. Money that is already spoken for, whether or not it has left the account yet. 3. What is still sitting with clients past their due date. Not the total receivables figure. The overdue portion. That is the number that turns into a cash problem if nobody is watching it. Run these three before you touch a single reply. In fifteen minutes you will know whether this is a week to chase collections, hold on a purchase, or move forward with confidence. Most owners never build this habit. Not because they are careless. Because nobody ever showed them what their numbers were actually telling them. Save this for your Monday morning finance check.
