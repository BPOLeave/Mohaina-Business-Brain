Selected Hook · Curiosity Gap
There's one number most UAE owners never check. It matters most.
There's one number most UAE owners never check. It matters most. Your bank balance can look healthy while the business underneath it is losing money. I see this more than owners think, and usually not until year-end, when it's already too late to fix quietly. Here are the 3 numbers I'd check every month, if the bank balance wasn't allowed to count. 1. Net profit, not bank balance. Revenue minus real costs, including VAT and CIT you owe but haven't paid yet. The account can be full of money that isn't actually yours. 2. Accounts receivable. What clients owe you, and how old it is. A client who "always pays eventually" is quietly funding their business with your cash flow, not theirs. 3. Cash flow timing. When money actually lands versus when it goes out. A big deposit this week can hide a gap that's coming in three weeks, and by then it's harder to fix. I think the bank balance is the easiest number to check and the least honest one. It shows you last month's decisions, dressed up as this month's health. We go through all three with every client, every month. Second eyes catch what one person checking their own balance never will. Save this for month-end. Check these three before you check the account. P.S. A full bank account and a profitable business are not always the same thing.
