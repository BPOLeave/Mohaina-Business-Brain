If your auditor has not started, the window is closing. 2. Approved auditor check Your auditor must be on the free zone's closed panel. Submit from a non-approved firm and the submission is rejected. Not a recommendation. A condition. 4. AML/CFT policy DNFBP obligations apply to more businesses in the UAE than most owners realise. If this is you, your policy must be in place and current. Not filed once and forgotten. I think most business owners in free zones know about the name change. What they miss is that the name change sits on top of a longer compliance list that is already running. Second eyes on your free zone position this week — not because something is wrong.
