Full Post · Framework-Heavy (Variation A)

Your bank balance is the least honest number in your business. I think most owners check it first thing in the morning. If it's healthy, they relax. If it's low, they panic. Either way, they think they know how the business is doing. They don't. Not really. A bank balance alone hides three numbers that actually tell you the truth. 1. What you're owed. Receivables sitting unpaid don't show up in your balance. I've seen an owner discover AED 800,000 in unpaid invoices he genuinely didn't know were there. His bank balance looked fine the whole time. 2. What you owe, and when. A healthy balance today can disappear the moment three supplier payments land in the same week. Cash in the bank isn't the same as cash you can actually spend. 3. What your margin is actually doing. Revenue can be climbing while your real margin quietly shrinks underneath it. If nobody's tracking it line by line, you won't see it until it's already a problem. We think like owners here. That's why we build reports that show what the numbers are actually telling you, not just what's sitting in the account. If you've never seen these three numbers laid out clearly, that's worth fixing before your next board or bank conversation. Save this for your next board or bank conversation. P.S. The bank balance tells you where you've been. These three numbers tell you where you're going.
