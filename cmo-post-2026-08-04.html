The AED 10,000 waiver closed 31 July. Most owners still think it covers them. I had a client last month who was sure he was fine. He registered for CIT the day the reminder email came in. In his mind, that was the box ticked. I think that's where most UAE owners get it wrong. Registering on time and being clear of the AED 10,000 late registration penalty are not the same thing. The waiver that covered late registrations closed on 31 July. If you're only realising that now, here are three things I'd check before you assume you're in the clear. First, check the actual registration date on your FTA portal, not the date you think you applied. We've seen submissions sit in draft for weeks before anyone noticed. Second, check whether your first CIT return was filed and the tax settled, not just registered. Registration is step one. The penalty clock doesn't stop until the return is filed and the amount owed is paid. Third, check who confirmed this for you. If it's the same person handling your bookkeeping, ask them to show you the FTA correspondence directly. We go through it ourselves before we tell a client they're clear, because a letter alone doesn't show you what actually happened. At the end of the day, "I registered on time" and "I'm covered" are two different sentences. Only one of them protects you. Book a 30-minute compliance review. P.S. The review takes 30 minutes. Sorting out a missed CIT penalty takes a lot longer.
