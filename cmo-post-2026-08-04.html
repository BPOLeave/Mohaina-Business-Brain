The CIT penalty waiver closed three days ago. I think most owners breathed a sigh of relief when they filed on time. But relief is exactly where the risk hides. Here's what we see, every single year, in the weeks right after a deadline passes: The pressure is gone. So the habit disappears with it. Filing on time isn't compliance. It's one moment inside compliance. What happens in the eleven months after is what actually protects you. The 4-step rhythm we run for every client, deadline or no deadline: 1. Documents reviewed monthly, not chased in December. If your supporting documents aren't verified as they come in, you won't catch the gap until it's too late to fix quietly. 2. Second eyes on every submission. One person doing the books and the filing means one person's blind spot becomes your exposure. 3. A real P&L, not a bank balance. Your bank balance tells you what's in the account. It doesn't tell you what's owed, what's coming, or what's already gone wrong. 4. A standing compliance calendar, reviewed monthly. VAT, CIT, AML, licence renewals. Not remembered. Tracked. We think like owners because if a client loses a dirham, it's something that also comes from our pocket too. The waiver is closed. The next deadline is already on the calendar, whether you've looked at it or not. Book a 30-minute compliance review.
