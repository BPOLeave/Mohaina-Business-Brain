There are two eInvoicing deadlines in the UAE. Most accounting software only talks about one of them. I think the confusion started because the first announcement focused on large businesses — companies with annual turnover above AED 150 million. They're required to connect to the FTA's system from 1 January 2027. That's the date that spread everywhere. But for the businesses I work with — UAE SMEs, most of them under AED 50 million — the live date is 1 July 2027. Six months later. But still this financial year. And here is the part most owners do not realise: the FTA's system is not plug-and-play. You cannot flip a switch on 30 June and call yourself compliant. The system requires a connected accounting platform that can generate and transmit structured electronic invoices in the FTA's PEPPOL-based format — every invoice you issue, from that date forward, automatically. If your accounting system is manual, outdated, or not PEPPOL-ready, you have about 11 months to fix it. That sounds long. A proper system implementation — Zoho Books or Odoo, configured and tested — takes three to six months minimum. Testing, data migration, and staff training come on top of that. We started getting these calls from clients who found out in the second half of 2025. The ones who called early had options. The ones who called in Q4 were making decisions under pressure. I am not saying this to push anyone. I am saying it because the window is smaller than it looks. If your business is on manual invoicing or a system you know is not PEPPOL-ready, the time to check is now. Not December. Book a 30-minute systems review. P.S. If you're already on Zoho Books or Odoo, we can check your configuration. It may need to be updated even if the system itself is fine.
⚠️ This post references UAE regulatory deadlines (1 Jan 2027 large businesses · 1 Jul 2027 SMEs under AED 50M) and the FTA's PEPPOL-based eInvoicing mandate. Verify with tax.gov.ae before approving.
