Full Post Text (1,443 characters)

AED 500. That's what a late Corporate Tax filing costs, for every month it stays late. I think most SME owners find out their accounting system can't actually answer a Corporate Tax question at the worst possible time. The Corporate Tax return deadline is 30 September. Thirty days away. Here's what "CT-ready" actually means, from what we see in client files. Your accounting system pulls a P&L and balance sheet without someone rebuilding it in Excel first. Every invoice, expense, and adjustment for the tax period is already reconciled, not sitting in a folder waiting to be sorted. Your bookkeeper can tell you your taxable income today, not after three days of digging. If any of that made you pause, you're not alone. Most of the SME owners we onboard are running Zoho Books, or a system that was set up once and never touched again. We go in, check the chart of accounts against what CIT actually requires, and fix what's missing before the deadline finds it for you. We don't just check the numbers on a screen. We go through the actual supporting documents, the way we do with every new client, because a system can look ready and still be missing something underneath. Thirty days is enough time. Thirty days after the deadline is not. DM me "CT-ready" and I'll tell you honestly whether your system would pass, or what it needs before it can. P.S. The FTA doesn't care that your system "used to work fine." Neither does a penalty.
