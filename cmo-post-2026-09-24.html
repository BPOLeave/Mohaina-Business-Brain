Cheap accounting has a new way of costing you money. I think most owners still measure the cost of accounting by the invoice they get each month. Not by what happens when it's wrong. Here's what changed. The UAE just tightened the rules again. Cabinet Decision No. 149 of 2026 restricts how much input tax you can recover on large cash payments, and changes how input tax apportionment gets calculated. If your bookkeeper hasn't updated your VAT process for this, your return is already off and neither of you knows it yet. This is the part that gets missed. A cheap accountant isn't cheap because the fee is low. They're cheap because their mistakes cost you more to find than the fee ever saved you. I've seen a business quoted AED 18,000 to reconstruct three years of accounts after their previous accountant left. Three years of decisions made on numbers nobody could stand behind. The rules keep moving. VAT apportionment, CIT, eInvoicing. Someone needs to be watching them move on your file, not learning about the change from a LinkedIn post two months late. Second eyes on your VAT position now cost a lot less than a reconstruction later. Tag a founder who needs to see this. P.S. The cheapest accountant was never the one with the lowest fee. It was the one whose mistakes cost the least to fix.
