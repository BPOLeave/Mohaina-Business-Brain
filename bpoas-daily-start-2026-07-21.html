Inside an Owner-Led Firm 4/4 Diagnostic Pass 🔴 Held — Awaiting Approval
He thought his biggest risk was a bad tenant. It was actually sitting in his inbox.
A client of ours owns a handful of properties across Dubai. Trading, renting, flipping when the market is right. He came to us to clean up his books. Nothing urgent, just standard onboarding. When we went through his documents, we found an FTA audit notice. It had been sitting in his email for almost a month. Unopened. He did not ignore it on purpose. He just did not know what it was. Property owners get a lot of mail. This one looked like every other notice he had scrolled past. But this one was different. It was an audit request tied to VAT on a commercial unit he had sold the year before. The response window was already closing. We opened it that same day. Pulled the transaction records, checked what was actually owed, and responded before the deadline ran out. I think most owners assume VAT is something that happens at filing time. It is not. It follows every transaction, every sale, every lease, whether you are watching for it or not. If you are the one checking your own email for something you do not know how to recognise, you are not protected. You just have not been caught yet. Second eyes matter. Not because you are careless. Because you are busy running a business, not reading FTA correspondence line by line. Tag a property investor who needs to see this.
