Post Copy — Ready to Publish

16 days. That is how long UAE business owners have to file their Corporate Tax return before the AED 500 per month administrative penalty begins. 30 September 2026 is the FTA deadline for Corporate Tax returns covering the financial year ending 31 December 2025. I see this every year. Business owners who kept reasonable records all year, who think they're fine — then discover in the last two weeks that their accountant has not registered them with the FTA, or that their books are missing three months of reconciliation, or that they have intercompany transactions that nobody has documented. The penalty is not the worst part. The worst part is the scramble. The late nights. The documents dug out of old email chains. The accountant who is suddenly unavailable. Here is what getting it right looks like, based on what we do for BPOAS clients every September: 1. Confirm your Tax Registration Number (TRN) is active and the period is registered with EmaraTax 2. Reconcile your books through 31 December 2025 — every bank account, not just the main operating account 3. Review related-party transactions and loans — the FTA looks at these first 4. Prepare a trial balance and check that it matches your VAT returns for the same period 5. Have your accountant or tax agent review the final return before submission — not after If you are not sure where you stand on any of these five points, that is where the risk is. The FTA does not accept "my accountant said so." They accept a clean, reviewed, verified return. Filed on time. We do a 30-minute compliance review for UAE SMEs — no charge, no obligation. If there is a gap, we tell you exactly what it is and what it takes to close it before the deadline. Book it this week, not next week. #CorporateTax #UAE #FTA #SME #Accounting #Compliance
