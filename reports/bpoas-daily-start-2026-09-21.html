Your CIT return is due in 9 days. Most owners think "ready" just means the return gets filed. Last month, a client called me four days before their deadline. Confident. "We're ready," they said. Your accountant has reviewed for any elections or positions that affect your liability Filing a return is the easy part. Getting the numbers right first — that's where the work is. The penalty for a late return is AED 500 per month. But the cost of filing an inaccurate return goes beyond the fine. 9 days is enough time to get this right, if you start today.
