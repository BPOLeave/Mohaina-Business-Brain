Managing Partner, BPO Accounting Services · now
You can look profitable on paper and still be bleeding. The owner feels fine. The books say something else, if anyone is reading them. One client of ours ran a construction company. His secretary kept the books on the side. He was busy. Sales were coming in. He assumed someone was watching the numbers. Busy looks like fine. For a long time, it can hide what is actually happening. Then we went to his office and checked the supporting documents in person. The picture changed. He had a large amount sitting in accounts receivable. Money he had already earned, and never collected. He could not see it. His books were never built to tell him. They did not lie to him. They just stayed quiet. We rebuilt three years of accounts. Once he could see clearly, he started collecting, and he started deciding with real information. That business went from 8 staff to more than 50. This is what clarity actually buys you. The ability to decide with facts instead of a feeling. And the FTA does not wait for you to catch up. Once a return is filed, you cannot change it. If you cannot say what your own numbers are telling you right now, that is the gap worth closing first. Message me the word "clarity" and I will tell you the first three things I would check in your books. P.S. We work like it is ours. When a client loses a dirham, it feels like it came from our own pocket.
