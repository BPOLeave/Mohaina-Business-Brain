"But a proper firm is too expensive." I hear this every week. It is backwards. The cheapest accountant is not the one with the lowest fee. It is the one whose mistakes cost you the least to fix. Here is what cheap accounting actually costs: Books nobody can stand behind. When your CT return is due and the numbers do not reconcile, you are not saving the fee. You are paying someone else to reconstruct the year. Penalties you did not have to pay. A missed tax invoice. A misclassified expense. VAT input claimed on something that does not qualify. Not dramatic failures. Small errors that compound quietly until the FTA asks for explanations. Decisions made on wrong numbers. You hired cheap accounting to run your business. But if the P&L you are reading is not accurate, every decision you make from it carries the same problem. I have seen reconstruction fees of AED 18,000 for three years of accounts. I have seen businesses pay more in FTA penalties than they spent on three years of the cheap firm. I have seen owners walk into their first CT filing with books that had to be rebuilt from bank statements. The cost was never the accounting fee. It was what happened when the accounting was wrong. Have you ever paid more to fix cheap work than doing it right would have cost? Tell me below.
