Cheap accounting has never once been cheap. It just delays the bill. I think most UAE SME owners believe they are fine because there is money in the bank. So they judge the whole business off one number, and they stop looking any further. A client came to me last year. Construction company. He had over AED 800,000 sitting in receivables he had no idea existed. His previous accountant never mentioned it. Not once, in three years. He was not broke. He just could not see it. That is the danger. Not the absence of money, the absence of visibility. Corporate Tax is in force now. Every UAE entity needs proper books, not a spreadsheet someone updates when they remember. eInvoicing is coming next, and it will not care if your bank balance looks healthy either. The FTA does not check your bank balance. It checks your records. We go to the office. We check the supporting documents physically. We run every file through more than one set of eyes before it reaches a client, because a mistake in a filing does not cost us anything close to what it costs the owner who signed it. At the end of the day, the real cost was never the accounting fee. It is what happens when the accounting is wrong, and by then it is a penalty, not a bill. If you are judging your business by your bank balance, this is your sign. Book a 30-minute compliance review. P.S. The AED 18,000 reconstruction I did last year could have been avoided with one proper review, three years earlier.
