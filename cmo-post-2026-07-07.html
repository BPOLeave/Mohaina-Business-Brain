Full Post · Copy-Ready

A trading company came to us with a stalled license renewal. The hold was not immigration. It was not the bank. It was not a missing document on their side. It was one line — three years old — in a VAT return nobody had looked at since it was filed. The return was technically submitted. The line was technically correct at the time it was entered. But the category had been reclassified. The system had moved on. The return had not. We found it in the first pass of the file. We corrected it. The renewal cleared within the week. Owner-led firms almost always have a version of this. Not fraud. Not negligence. A file that was accurate when it was written and quietly wrong by the time it mattered. Save this for your next license renewal.
