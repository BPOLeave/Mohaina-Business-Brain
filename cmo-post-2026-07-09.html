Filing on time does not mean you are protected. It just means nobody has looked closely yet. We see this a lot. An owner files VAT every quarter, files CIT on schedule, never misses a deadline. On paper, they look clean. Then an audit comes, or a bank asks for supporting documents, or the FTA asks a follow up question. And the gap shows up. Compliant means you submitted the right form on the right date. Audit-ready means every number on that form can be traced back to a document that proves it is true. Those are not the same thing. Most UAE SME owners only find out the difference the hard way, sitting across from an auditor asking questions their own bookkeeper never asked. We see this constantly. One client's returns were filed correctly for three years. Nobody had checked whether the input tax claimed on every one of them was actually claimable, or whether the invoices behind it would hold up. Filing on time had covered the surface. It had not built what was underneath. Second eyes matter here. One person filing a return is not the same as a team checking that return against the actual paperwork before it goes anywhere near the FTA portal. If your accountant has never once asked to see the invoice behind a number, that is worth noticing now, not during an audit. Tag a founder who needs to see this.
