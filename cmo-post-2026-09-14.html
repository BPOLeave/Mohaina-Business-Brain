There's a UAE tax deadline in two weeks that most owners haven't even started preparing for. I think most owners have 30 September written down somewhere, but haven't actually looked at what needs to happen before it gets here. If your tax period ended 31 December 2025, your Corporate Tax return and payment are due by 30 September 2026. That's nine months from year end, under Federal Decree-Law No. 47 of 2022. Two weeks away now, not two months. Here's what we check before that date, every single time. 1. The accounting profit in your financial statements matches what actually gets filed. Your return starts from your own books, not from what you think your profit was. 2. Related-party transactions are documented, not just remembered. If it's not on paper, it doesn't count as compliant, no matter how well you remember the arrangement. 3. Small Business Relief eligibility is confirmed, not assumed. Owners assume they qualify and find out otherwise after the deadline, when it's too late to fix anything. None of this is complicated. It just needs someone to sit with your numbers before the date, not after it. We think like owners here, so if a client misses this, it feels like our mistake too. That's why we start the conversation weeks before the deadline, not the week of it. Book a 30-minute compliance review. P.S. The review takes 30 minutes. An FTA query about a missed return takes a lot longer than that.
