Extend — UAE-Specific Compliance Reference ⚠️ Verify before posting
For companies with a 31 December 2025 financial year-end, the Corporate Tax return and payment are due by 30 September 2026 under Federal Decree-Law No. 47 of 2022 (Article 51). Late payment of Corporate Tax carries a penalty of 14% per annum under Cabinet Decision 129/2025, effective 14 April 2026. That penalty starts from the due date. Not the date you realise you missed it. Three months is accurate. But only if you use them.

September 30 is the Corporate Tax filing deadline for most UAE companies.


Most owners I speak to think they are fine.


I look at their books. They are not fine.


Here is what "ready" actually requires. Not what it sounds like it requires.


Not a clean VAT history. VAT compliance and CIT readiness are two different standards. Filing your VAT returns on time does not mean your books are ready for a Corporate Tax calculation.


Not your accountant saying so. I have seen books where the accountant believed everything was correct. Nobody had gone through the source documents. Nobody had reconciled revenue to the VAT returns. The numbers looked tidy. They were not defensible.


Ready actually means this.


Your full-year close is done. Every significant expense has a valid tax invoice behind it. Related-party transactions (internal loans, management fees, director dealings) are documented correctly. Revenue reconciles to your VAT filings. You can produce a tax calculation you would stand behind if the FTA started asking questions.


Most owners do not know their books are not ready until someone goes in and looks.


We go in and look. No remote review. No assumptions. We physically go to the office and check the source documents. That is the job.


Three months to September 30 sounds like enough time. It is not if you are starting from where most UAE SMEs are today.


DM me CITREADY and I will tell you exactly where your books stand before September.
