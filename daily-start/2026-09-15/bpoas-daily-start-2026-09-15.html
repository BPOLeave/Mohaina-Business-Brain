Today's LinkedIn Post
I think "it's been handled" are the most expensive words. Not "I'm not sure." Not "I made a mistake." Those are fixable. "Handled" means you stopped asking. A professional services firm came to us earlier this year. Good revenue, growing team, two years of books managed by the same bookkeeper. Every VAT question from the owner got the same answer: it's all sorted, it's handled. We went on-site. Not to audit the software. To check the actual supporting documents. I think what we found is a common story in professional services. Input tax claims had been processed for two years against invoices that didn't meet the FTA's format requirements. Not a single invoice at a time. Two years of them, compounding quietly. The owner didn't know this was even a risk. The bookkeeper didn't know either. That's not a character flaw. That's the gap between entering numbers and understanding what the FTA actually needs to see. That gap only closes when someone physically checks the paper. At the end of the day, the FTA doesn't ask about the software. They ask for the invoice. Save this for the situation where your bookkeeper says "it's handled." P.S. Book a 30-minute compliance review. The FTA query takes considerably longer.
