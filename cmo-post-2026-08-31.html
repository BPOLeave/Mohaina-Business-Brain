Full Post

I think most SME owners will hear about eInvoicing for the first time from their IT vendor, not their accountant. That's the problem.

We've started having this conversation with clients preparing for CIT and VAT filings, and eInvoicing keeps coming up as an afterthought. Phase 1 rolls out 30 October. It is not a software upgrade. It is a compliance requirement tied directly to your VAT and CIT filings.

Here's what it actually means for a UAE SME.

Your invoicing system has to talk to the FTA portal directly. Not export a report. Not email a PDF. Connect.

If your current system cannot do that, someone has to fix it before the deadline, not after the first missed filing.

We think like owners, so here's how we'd frame it if it were our own business.

Know which invoices, in and out, are in scope.
Confirm your accounting system can actually integrate, not just claim to.
Test the connection before the deadline, not on it.

I've watched clients treat this exactly like they treated CIT eighteen months ago, assuming there was more time than there was. There wasn't then. There won't be now.

At the end of the day, the FTA doesn't care whose department this fell under. It cares whether the invoice went through.

P.S. Book a 30-minute compliance review before the FTA finds the gap for you.
