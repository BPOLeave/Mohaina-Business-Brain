The UAE just moved an eInvoicing deadline. Most SME owners still haven't heard about it. I think most owners hear "eInvoicing mandate" and assume it doesn't apply to them yet, so they stop reading. Here's what changed. The Ministry of Finance pushed the deadline for appointing an Accredited Service Provider, the ASP, from 31 July to 30 October 2026. Right now that applies to businesses turning over above AED 50 million, fully compliant by 1 January 2027. Under that threshold? Not your deadline yet. But three things are worth knowing before it is. 1. The mandate rolls out in phases, not all at once. Large businesses go first. The rest follow on a timeline the FTA hasn't finalized. "Not yet" isn't "not coming." 2. ASP capacity is limited. As of August, the Ministry lists 50 accredited and pre-approved providers total. When the mandate widens to thousands of SMEs, slots won't be instant. 3. Your accounting system needs to be ready before your ASP is. An ASP connects to your invoicing data. If your books aren't structured to produce clean, compliant invoices, the ASP can't fix that for you. We're already getting clients' Zoho and Odoo systems ready, months early. Second eyes on your invoicing setup now costs a lot less than a rushed appointment later. DM me "ASP-ready" and I'll walk you through what your business needs. P.S. The businesses moving early aren't doing it because the FTA is asking. They're doing it because waiting has a cost too.
