The eInvoicing deadline got pushed to October. Most owners think that is good news.

The Ministry of Finance moved the deadline to appoint an Accredited Service Provider to 30 October 2026. A lot of owners read that as extra time. I think it is the opposite. It is the same deadline, just later on the calendar. The actual go-live for Phase 1 is still 1 January 2027, and that date has not moved at all.

A client asked us last month if the extension meant the eInvoicing conversation could wait until next year. It cannot. An ASP has to be appointed. Your accounting system has to talk to that ASP. Your invoices have to move in a format the FTA can read automatically, not a PDF someone emails across.

If you are still running paper invoices, Excel, or a system that was never built to connect to anything, this is not a project you start in December and finish by January.

We have moved clients onto Zoho Books and Odoo before, and the system change always takes longer than the deadline feels like it allows. The accounting side has to be ready before the pressure hits, not after.

Compliance you can stand behind starts with a system that is actually built for it, not one you are patching together in the last few weeks.

DM me "eInvoice-ready" and I will walk you through what Phase 1 actually requires for your business.

P.S. 30 October sounds far away. It is 51 days from today.
