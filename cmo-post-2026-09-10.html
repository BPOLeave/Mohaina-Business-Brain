The cheapest accountant we've ever met cost one client AED 18,000 to fix.

He ran a construction company. His accounting was a secretary, doing it alongside her actual job, on a system nobody had properly set up. He never asked for financials. He watched his bank balance and called that visibility.

I think that's how most owners end up here. Not because they don't care about their numbers, but because a cheap bookkeeper feels like the safe, boring choice. Nothing goes wrong until the day it does. Until it does.

By the time he came to us, three years of accounts needed to be rebuilt from the ground up. Invoices to match. Receivables nobody had chased. Payments that were never reconciled. A picture of the business that had never actually existed on paper. The reconstruction alone ran AED 18,000, and that was before he even knew what his real numbers looked like.

That is not the cost of an accountant. That is the cost of not having one.

We think like owners here, so we would rather tell a client the truth about their books today than let them find out the hard way in three years. A secretary can be excellent at her job and still not be an accountant. Those are two different jobs, and UAE compliance does not care which one you thought you were paying for.

Tag a founder who needs to see this.

P.S. Cheap accounting doesn't disappear. It just waits.
