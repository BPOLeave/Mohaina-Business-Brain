I had a client come to us last year. He'd been filing his own VAT returns for two years. Confident. Efficient. Saving money. Then we went to his office. Checked the supporting documents. Found input tax being claimed on invoices that didn't meet FTA requirements. Nothing malicious. Just a gap in what he knew. We submitted a voluntary disclosure before the FTA found it themselves. The penalty was a fraction of what it would have been. But here's what I think about more than the number: He had no idea the exposure existed. None. That's not his fault. He's running a business. He's not a tax specialist. The risk doesn't go away because you don't see it. It just waits. If your accountant has never physically checked your supporting documents, that's worth a conversation this week. #UAE #VAT #FTA #SmallBusiness #AccountingUAE #BPOAS #Compliance
