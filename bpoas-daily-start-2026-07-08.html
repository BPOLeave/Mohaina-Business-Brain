⏳ PENDING APPROVAL Wednesday · Soft-Sell Framework · "What the Numbers Are Telling You" pillar
I used to tell clients: if the bank balance looks healthy, don't worry. I was wrong, and it cost some of them more than they knew. Ask any UAE SME owner how the business is doing and most will check one thing: the bank balance. I get it. It is right there. It updates in real time. It feels like the truth. It is not the truth. It is one number, at one moment, after some invoices are paid and before others land. Owners hire off that number. Owners hold off on hiring off that number too. Big mistake either way if it is the only thing you are reading. Here is the one-line gut check we walk clients through: Bank balance tells you what you have right now. Profit tells you what the business actually earned. The gap between them is everything still moving: money owed to you, money you owe, stock sitting on a shelf, a VAT payment due next week. A business can hold AED 200,000 in the account and be losing money every month. Another can show AED 12,000 and be genuinely profitable, just waiting on invoices to land. Three questions to run this week: 1. What did you actually invoice this month, paid or not? 2. What do you owe that has not left the account yet? 3. Strip both out of the bank figure. What is left? That number, not the bank app, is the one that tells you if the business is working. Most owners have never run this gut check. Not because they are careless. Because nobody ever showed them where to look. DM me CASHFLOW and I'll walk you through what your numbers are actually telling you.
