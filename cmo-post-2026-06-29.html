If your company is registered in DMCC, tomorrow — 30 June — is the last day to update your company name ending to FZCO. After that, you are out of step with the Registrar. While you have this open — here are 4 other free zone compliance checks to run before the week is out: 1. Audited financial statements All major Dubai free zones now require audited accounts for licence renewal. DMCC, DAFZA, DIFC, JAFZA. Applies to financial years starting 1 June 2023 onwards. If your auditor has not started, the window is closing. 2. Approved auditor check Your auditor must be on the free zone's closed panel. Submit from a non-approved firm and the submission is rejected. Not a warning. A rejection. 3. QFZP — the 0% Corporate Tax condition If your entity is claiming the Qualifying Free Zone Person rate, IFRS-compliant audited accounts are a condition of that rate. Not a recommendation. A condition. 4. AML/CFT policy DNFBP obligations apply to more businesses in the UAE than most owners realise. If this is you, your policy must be in place and current. Not filed once and forgotten. I think most business owners in free zones know about the name change. What they miss is that the name change sits on top of a longer compliance list that is already running. Second eyes on your free zone position this week — not because something is wrong. Because something could be. Save this. Tag a founder in a UAE free zone who needs to see it before tomorrow midnight. ⚠️

3 Pinned Comments

Pinned 1 — DMCC name transition detail
The FZCO suffix has been mandatory for new DMCC companies since 2 January 2025. Existing companies have had the transition period. 30 June closes that window. If you are not sure whether this applies to your entity, check your trade licence today. The update is done through the DMCC member portal — it is not complex. The risk is missing the deadline, not completing the process.
Pinned 2 — Approved auditor panel
The free zone approved auditor list is one of the most common surprises we see. A business owner engages an accountant, gets the accounts done, submits for licence renewal — and is told the auditor is not on the panel. The submission is rejected. This wastes months and creates a real licence renewal risk. Check the approved auditor list before you sign the engagement, not after.
Pinned 3 — DNFBP / AML-CFT
On AML/CFT obligations: this catches more businesses than people expect. If your business handles real estate transactions, precious metals, precious stones, or certain professional services, you may already be a Designated Non-Financial Business or Profession under UAE law. The AML/CFT policy is not complex to put in place. But it has to exist, be documented, and be followed. If you are not sure whether this applies to you — that is the first question to answer this week.
