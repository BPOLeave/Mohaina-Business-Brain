Step 2 · LinkedIn Post · Framework Post (Variation A)
If your company is registered in DMCC, stop. Tomorrow, 30 June, is the last day to update your company name ending to FZCO. After that, you are out of step with the Registrar. Most owners have not done it. And most do not know the 4 other things they should be checking this week too. While you have this open: 1. DMCC FZCO name change: deadline 30 June 2026 The DMCC Registrar requires all existing companies to transition their name suffix to FZCO. The window for existing companies closes tomorrow. 2. Audited financial statements for licence renewal All major Dubai free zones, DMCC, DAFZA, DIFC, JAFZA, require audited accounts for licence renewal for financial years starting 1 June 2023 onward. No audit, no renewal. 3. Approved auditor check Your auditor must be on the free zone's closed panel. If they are not, the submission is rejected automatically. This one catches people off guard. 4. QFZP status and the 0% Corporate Tax rate To qualify for the 0% CT rate as a Qualifying Free Zone Person, you need IFRS-compliant audited accounts. Not management accounts. Audited. 5. AML/CFT policy: current and documented If your business falls under DNFBP obligations, your policy must be current. This is not a one-time setup. It needs to be reviewed and updated. Five checks. One week. Save this + tag a founder in a UAE free zone who needs to see this before tomorrow midnight.

Comment 1
Extend — UAE-specific compliance detail · QFZP substance test
One thing worth adding: the QFZP 0% Corporate Tax rate is not automatic even with IFRS-compliant accounts. You also need to meet the Qualifying Income conditions and the adequate substance test for your specific free zone. If your auditor is not actively tracking this, you may appear to qualify but lose the rate in an FTA review. Second eyes on this before September is worth the call.
