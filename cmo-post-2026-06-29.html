While you have this open, here are 4 more free zone compliance checks to run before the week is out. 1. Audited accounts for licence renewal Dubai free zones including DMCC, DAFZA, DIFC and JAFZA now require audited financial statements for licence renewal, for financial years starting 1 June 2023 onward. If your auditor has not started, you are already behind. 2. Your auditor must be on the approved panel Every free zone keeps a closed list of approved auditors. If yours is not on it, the submission gets rejected automatically. Check before you book. 3. QFZP status requires IFRS-compliant audited accounts If you are claiming the 0% Corporate Tax rate as a Qualifying Free Zone Person, your accounts need to be audited and IFRS-compliant. No exceptions under the CT Law. 4. AML/CFT policy: current and documented If you fall under DNFBP obligations, your AML/CFT policy must be in place and up to date. An outdated document counts the same as no document when the regulator asks. Compliance is not one thing. It is everything running at once. Save this for your next licence review. Comment 1 — Extend (UAE compliance stat + law reference)

Quick note on the DMCC FZCO requirement: the obligation aligns with UAE Federal Law No. 32 of 2021 on Commercial Companies. FZCO stands for Free Zone Company, the new mandatory designation replacing FZE for most DMCC entities. The Registrar has been processing name updates since early 2025. If you have not submitted the request yet, the DMCC member portal is the starting point. DMCC is the UAE's largest free zone by company count, with over 24,000 registered businesses. Most will not have seen this coming.
