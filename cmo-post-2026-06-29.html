If your company is registered in DMCC, tomorrow, 30 June, is the last day to update your company name ending to FZCO. After that, you are out of step with the Registrar. While you have this open, 4 other free zone compliance checks to run this week. 1. AUDITED FINANCIAL STATEMENTS FOR LICENCE RENEWAL If your financial year started on or after 1 June 2023, you need audited financial statements to renew your licence in DMCC, DAFZA, DIFC, or JAFZA. Not management accounts. Not a summary. Audited. If your renewal is coming up and this is not done, it is already late. 2. YOUR AUDITOR MUST BE ON THE FREE ZONE'S APPROVED PANEL You can have a licensed, qualified auditor and still get your submission rejected. Free zones only accept auditors from their own closed panel. Check the panel before you start the engagement, not after. 3. QFZP STATUS AND YOUR CIT RATE Claiming the 0% qualifying income rate under UAE Corporate Tax? Your accounts must be IFRS-compliant and your financial statements must be audited. No audited accounts means no QFZP. No QFZP means the 0% rate does not apply. 4. AML/CFT POLICY, CURRENT AND DOCUMENTED If your business is classified as a DNFBP, your AML/CFT policy must be in place and current. A 2022 policy that has never been reviewed does not count. Compliance is not one thing running in the background. It is five things running at once. Most owners only find out one is broken when the others are already late. Comment 1 · Extend · UAE Law Reference
A note on the QFZP condition. Under UAE Corporate Tax Law, a Qualifying Free Zone Person must maintain audited financial statements prepared in accordance with IFRS or IFRS for SMEs. The FTA has no mechanism to accept an unaudited submission from a business claiming the 0% rate. This is not a technicality. If your financials were not audited for the year you are filing, the QFZP claim does not stand. Book a 30-minute compliance review if you are not sure where your CIT position sits.
