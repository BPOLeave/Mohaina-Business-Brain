Your CIT return is due in 9 days. Most owners think "ready" just means the return gets filed. I think that's the wrong bar. A client called us in August, certain their books were fine. Their bookkeeper closed the accounts every month, on time, no complaints. We went through the supporting documents anyway. Second eyes. That's when we found AED 90,000 in invoices that had never been reconciled against the bank statements. Filed as-is, the numbers would have been wrong. Not fraudulent, just wrong. And once it's submitted on the FTA portal, you cannot quietly go back and fix it. Here's what "CIT-ready" actually means before 30 September: 1. Your trial balance is reconciled against your bank statements, not just closed on time. 2. Every invoice has a supporting document behind it, not just a number in the system. 3. Someone who didn't prepare the books has reviewed them. 4. You know your tax liability before the FTA tells you what it is. Miss the deadline and it's an AED 500 penalty every month after, not once. At the end of the day, a CIT return isn't a formality. It's the one document that tells the government what your business actually did all year. Book a 30-minute compliance review. We'll tell you if you're actually ready, not just on time. P.S. The review takes 30 minutes. An FTA query takes a lot longer.
