Full Post · Copy-Ready

The cheapest accountant you can hire is rarely the cheapest accounting you will ever pay for. I think most owners look at the fee and stop there. Cheap invoice, good deal. That's the whole comparison, and it's the wrong one. We picked up a client last year whose books had never actually been checked. His accountant was fine on paper. Filed on time, kept the ledger updated, never missed a deadline. The owner had no reason to doubt him. But nobody had gone back and verified three years of transactions against the real supporting documents, not just the ledger totals. When we did, we found the mess. AED 18,000 was what it cost just to reconstruct three years of accounts before we could even start doing the job properly. That number never showed up on the old accountant's invoice. It showed up later, right when the owner needed to trust his numbers for a CIT filing and couldn't. I think this is the part owners miss. A cheap accountant who is capable in some ways can still be dangerous, because he doesn't know what he doesn't know. And if you're the owner, you don't either. Nobody is checking his work. Second eyes are not a luxury. They are what catches the gap before the FTA does, not after. At the end of the day, the real cost of cheap accounting is never the invoice. It's the reconstruction you didn't know you would need. Tag a founder who needs to see this.
