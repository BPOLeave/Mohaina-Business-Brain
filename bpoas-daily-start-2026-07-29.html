A client called us last month to ask if their current accounting system would be compatible with the UAE's eInvoicing mandate. They've been on a spreadsheet for three years. I didn't know where to start. Here's what's happening. The UAE is introducing mandatory eInvoicing for all businesses. If your revenue is over AED 50 million, you need to appoint an Accredited Service Provider by 30 October 2026. For everyone else, 1 January 2027 is the date. That sounds far away. It isn't. Because the issue isn't the mandate. The issue is what happens when you try to connect a system that isn't ready. If your accounting software wasn't built for this — or if you're still doing everything manually — the integration doesn't happen overnight. It takes setup time, data cleaning, and at least one round of errors you didn't anticipate. We've been implementing Zoho Books and Odoo for UAE SMEs for a while now. Both are eInvoicing-ready. But the clients who came to us a month before deadline had a much harder time than the ones who came six months out. If you're not sure whether your current system can handle this, now is a good time to check. Not because the deadline is tomorrow. Because fixing it takes longer than most people think. What does your current accounting system look like?
