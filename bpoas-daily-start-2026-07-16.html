Today's LinkedIn Post — Copy Ready · Pillar: The True Cost of Cheap Accounting
Framework: Contrarian Take · Hook: The Cost Reframe · Score: 4/4 · ~1,350 chars · Publish: 08:30 UAE
I think a good accountant should make you uncomfortable sometimes. If yours never does, that is not necessarily a good sign. We went on-site for a new client last year. Construction business, Dubai. He had the same accountant for three years. "No issues," he told me. "Very smooth relationship." We spent half a day reviewing supporting documents. His input tax claims had been filed identically for 36 months. No one had ever asked whether the invoices actually met FTA requirements. They did not. His accountant was agreeable. Easy to work with. Never caused a problem. Also never told him anything he did not want to hear. I think that is the real cost of cheap accounting. Not the fee you pay. It is the conversation your accountant avoided having with you. The FTA can go back five years. The previous accountant's silence does not protect you from that. A good accountant's job is to tell you what the numbers are actually saying, before someone else does. That sometimes means an uncomfortable call. A number you did not expect. A claim that needs reversing. At the end of the day, a comfortable relationship with your accountant and a protected business are not the same thing. What is one thing your accountant has never told you? Comment below. P.S. A 30-minute compliance review is the most valuable uncomfortable conversation your business can have this quarter.
