Most owners think a quiet accountant is a good accountant. I think that is backwards. I had a client in construction who came to us with a secretary handling the books alongside her actual job. Everything looked fine on paper. No one had flagged anything in three years. That was the problem, not the proof that things were fine. When we went through the supporting documents ourselves, we found a reconstruction job waiting, three years of it, and it cost him AED 18,000 to fix what should have been caught the first year. The old setup never told him anything was wrong because no one was actually checking. I think that is the real difference between a cheap accountant and a proper one. A cheap accountant tells you what you want to hear, or says nothing at all. A proper one tells you the thing you would rather not deal with, while there is still time to deal with it. We do not see our job as keeping you comfortable. We see it as catching what you cannot see yourself, the input tax that should not be claimed, the receivable no one is chasing, the registration that is quietly overdue, before the FTA is the one who tells you. At the end of the day, the accountant who never says anything difficult is not protecting you. They are just delaying the moment someone else does. Tag a founder who needs to see this.
