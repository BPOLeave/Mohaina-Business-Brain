Here's the filing sequence that actually gets you the waiver, and the mistakes we keep seeing owners make in the final week. Confirm your registration is filed, not just started. Owners who file something quick to "beat the deadline" and plan to amend later often lose the waiver on a technicality. Match the return to your real first tax period.
