There are 4 days left to avoid a penalty most business owners do not even know they are exposed to. I think this is the one that catches people off guard. If your company registered late for Corporate Tax, the FTA gave a window to fix it without a fine. That window closes 31 July. We had a client this month who registered late and did not know the waiver even existed. He assumed the AED 10,000 penalty was already sitting there, unavoidable. It was not. He just needed the return filed and the tax settled before the deadline, and it was done in time. That is the pattern we keep seeing. Owners either do not know a waiver exists, or they know about it and assume someone else is tracking the date. Neither is a plan. After 31 July, the FTA is not obligated to look the other way again. Here is what actually matters before Friday. Check whether your CIT registration date was late. Confirm your return is filed, not just started. Confirm the tax due is actually settled, not just calculated. Three different things, and we have seen owners assume all three were done when only one was. None of this shows up until someone looks. We think like owners here, so we do not wait for a penalty notice to tell us what should have been caught in June. Book a 30-minute compliance review.
