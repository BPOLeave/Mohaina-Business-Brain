Full Post · Copy-Ready
The real cost of cheap accounting isn't the FTA penalty. It's the three years you spent thinking your business was profitable.I see it all the time. A business owner calls us in. They've had the same accountant for three years. Low fee, no complaints. But when we sit down and actually check the books — the VAT returns, the supporting documents, the actual entries — what we find is different from what they were told. The penalty is the visible cost. Everyone sees that part. What they don't see is the wrong decision made on wrong numbers. The expansion that didn't make sense. The receivables that went uncollected because nobody was tracking them. The VAT input tax claimed incorrectly for three years — now a known submission with the FTA. That's the real cost. I think most people believe accounting is about compliance. Tick the box, file the return, move on. It's not. Accounting is about what the numbers are telling you — and whether the person reading them can actually hear it. If a client loses a dirham, it's something that also comes from our pocket. That's why we show up to their office. We check the invoices physically. We verify the supporting documents. We don't just trust what's already in the system. When it's in the FTA portal, you cannot change it again. So second eyes matter — before it gets there. The fee for cheap accounting is low. The cost is not.
