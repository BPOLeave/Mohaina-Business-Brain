Full Post Text (1,432 characters)

A consultancy's bank asked for two years of VAT returns before approving their growth facility. That's when we found the entertainment claims. Their bookkeeper had been claiming input tax on client dinners and gifts for two years. Under UAE VAT rules, entertainment expenses are specifically excluded from input tax recovery, no matter how legitimate the business reason behind them. Nobody had checked. The bookkeeper called it handled. We went through the returns line by line against the actual invoices, the way we do with every new client. AED 21,000 in input tax had been claimed on expenses that were never recoverable in the first place. I think most owners hear "VAT is filed" and assume that means "VAT is correct." It is not. Filed on time and filed right are two different things, and only one of them holds up when someone asks questions. We corrected two years of returns and filed the voluntary disclosure before the bank's review turned into an FTA problem. The facility went through. The exposure did not carry into it. If a client loses a dirham to something we should have caught, that comes from our side too. That's why we check what was actually claimed, not just what was filed. Save this for the situation where your bookkeeper says "it's handled." Ask what they actually checked. P.S. A bank isn't the only one who asks for your VAT history. An FTA audit does the same thing, just without the warning.
